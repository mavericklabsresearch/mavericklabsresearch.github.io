✦ Maverick Labs AI and Robotics LLC

A digital wonder that grows around each child — not the other way round.

Maverick Labs AI and Robotics is developing and experimentally validating a new approach to individualized assistive intelligence. Our primary initiative is MAVY — a personalized synthetic intelligence for individualized assistance to people with disabilities, originally conceived for children who are both blind and autistic, and designed to broaden across physical, sensory, developmental, and communication disabilities.

This work is at an early research and feasibility stage. Central capabilities have not yet been validated, and this page will say so until they have been.

✦ The premiseThe child is the center of the design

MAVY's core premise: the intelligence persists and develops through experience, while the underlying models, hardware, and embodiments are interchangeable. The child does not adapt to the machine. The machine learns the child's world — their senses, their pace, their way of communicating — and grows there.

Pillar One

Built around the child

Every profile is individual by design: assistance shaped to one person's senses, communication, and requirements — not a generic tool with settings.

Pillar Two

It persists, and grows

The intelligence is designed to keep what it has verified — across shutdowns, model swaps, and new hardware — and to keep developing through experience.

Pillar Three

Safety stands apart

Generative intelligence is kept separate from deterministic safety controls, so the part that imagines can never overrule the part that protects.

These pillars describe the intended architecture and the research programme testing it. They are design commitments, not validated results.
