✦ About us

A small lab with one big question: can intelligence grow around a child?

Maverick Labs AI and Robotics LLC is a research company in Oak Grove, Missouri. We are developing and experimentally validating a new approach to individualized assistive intelligence — technology designed to support people with disabilities, beginning with children who are both blind and autistic, and broadening across physical, sensory, developmental, and communication disabilities.

✦ Who we areThe people

Maverick Labs is led by its Chief Technologist, Tony Vidlak, working with collaborators and reviewers across the assistive-technology and machine-intelligence fields. The lab's habit, borrowed from good experimental practice anywhere: small, carefully measured studies, criteria fixed in advance, and results — including failures — on the record.

✦ Where we honestly areEarly research stage

Our primary initiative, MAVY, is at an early research and feasibility stage. Its central capabilities have not yet been validated. A project pitch describing the research programme has been submitted to the National Science Foundation's SBIR/STTR programme; funding has not been established as awarded. We would rather tell you that plainly than dress a research plan up as a product.
