✦ Maverick Labs AI and Robotics LLC

A digital wonder that grows around each child — not the other way round.

Maverick Labs AI and Robotics is developing and experimentally validating a new approach to individualized assistive intelligence. Our primary initiative is MAVY — a personalized synthetic intelligence for individualized assistance to people with disabilities, originally conceived for children who are both blind and autistic, and designed to broaden across physical, sensory, developmental, and communication disabilities.

This site exists to explain — in plain language, with both sides shown — what assistive AI can do for children, what it cannot, and what we are doing about the difference. This work is at an early research and feasibility stage; central capabilities have not yet been validated, and we will keep saying so until they have been.

✦ The premiseThe child is the center of the design

MAVY's core premise: the intelligence persists and develops through experience, while the underlying models, hardware, and embodiments are interchangeable. The child does not adapt to the machine. The machine learns the child's world — their senses, their pace, their way of communicating — and grows there. More about MAVY →

Pillar One

Built around the child

Assistance shaped to one person's senses, communication, and requirements — not a generic tool with settings.

Pillar Two

It persists, and grows

Designed to keep what it has verified — across shutdowns, model swaps, and new hardware — and to keep developing through experience.

Pillar Three

Safety stands apart

Generative intelligence kept separate from deterministic safety controls, so the part that imagines can never overrule the part that protects. Our safety principles →

These pillars describe the intended architecture and the research programme testing it. They are design commitments, not validated results.

✦ The researchShared, with its stage attached

Our programme runs in five research areas, supported by the open experiments of our sister institute, Substrate Research. Pitches, stages, and sources are on the Research page — including what has not been funded, validated, or demonstrated yet.
