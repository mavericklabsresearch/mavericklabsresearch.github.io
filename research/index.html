✦ Our programmeThe MAVY research areas

MAVY's programme runs in five areas — environmental learning and hardware adaptation; persistent intelligence and experience retention; embodied robotics and sensorimotor learning; an independent safety architecture; and individualized, disability-adaptive assistance. A project pitch describing this programme was submitted to the NSF SBIR/STTR programme in November 2026; funding has not been established as awarded. Detail on each area: MAVY.

✦ Reading furtherSources we trust

Policy · UNICEF

Guidance on AI and Children

The leading international guidance on child-centred AI: ten requirements covering safety, privacy, fairness, transparency, and inclusion, grounded in the Convention on the Rights of the Child. The standard we measure ourselves against.

Families · American Academy of Pediatrics

12 tips for healthy digital habits

Practical, non-alarmist guidance for families on children's digital life — media plans, screen-free times, and using technology together.

✦ How we shareOur rules for research information

Criteria before experiments, results after, failures at the same size as passes, and plain language first with technical records attached. When that standard slips, the record of changes on the relevant page is where we'll say so.

How to read this page. Everything here is educational information about a field, written in plain language. It is not medical advice, and it is not a claim about any product — including ours. MAVY is at an early research stage and its central capabilities have not yet been validated.
