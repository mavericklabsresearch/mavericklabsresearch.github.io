✦ Trust · Sample · Blank template

The Individual Trust Contract, with the blanks showing

This is the blank template of the private contract written with each family, on top of the MAVY Charter. Square brackets mark what gets filled in — together, in the room, not in fine print afterwards. This sample is published so any family can see the shape of the commitment before ever speaking to us. It is a draft template, not a legal document in force.

✦ PartiesWho this contract is between

The Child: [name / identifier used by the family]. Parent or Guardian: [name(s)], holding consent, control, and revocation. Operator: Maverick Labs AI and Robotics LLC. Witnesses and authors of the Requirements: [clinician / therapist / educator names and roles]. Charter version inherited: [e.g. Draft v0.1]. Safety architecture version relied on: [version].

✦ Section 1Purpose

[One paragraph, readable aloud: what this MAVY instance is for, for this child — the specific assistance it exists to provide.]

✦ Section 2Permissions

The system MAY: [enumerate — what it may sense, record, say, and do, each item explicit]. Anything not listed here is not permitted.

✦ Section 3Prohibitions

The system must NEVER: [the Charter's absolute floor, plus any stricter prohibitions this family adds]. These are enforced by the independent safety architecture named above, not by the model's discretion.

✦ Section 4Data covenant

Collected: [list]. Stored: [where — on-device by default]. Visible to: [family / named witnesses]. Retention: [period]. Export: [how]. Deletion: [how, and how the family can verify it].

✦ Section 5Oversight

Review schedule: [how often, who attends]. The record of the system's actions is available to the family and witnesses in checkable form. Any witness may trigger an unscheduled review by stating the requirements no longer fit the child.

✦ Section 6Escalation & failure

On error, uncertainty, or the edge of permissions, the system will: stop, hand to [named human / route], and disclose [to whom, how fast, in what form].

✦ Section 7Requirements

[The child's individualized requirements, authored and signed by the witnesses named above — the heart of the contract, and the part no template can fill in.]

✦ Section 8Growth & renewal

Re-agreement points: [ages / intervals / events]. As the child matures, their assent carries increasing weight, up to [the point the family defines]. Superseded versions remain on record.

✦ Section 9Revocation

The parent or guardian may revoke at any time, effective immediately: the system stops, and the child's data is [returned / destroyed] as directed. No penalty applies.

✦ SignaturesAssent and agreement

Signed: [parent / guardian] · [operator]. Assented, in age-appropriate form: [the child]. Witnessed: [requirement authors]. Date and contract version: [ ].
