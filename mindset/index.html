✦ The mindset · Before the machine

MAVY is a mindset before it is a machine

It is a way of thinking about assistive intelligence that starts from the person and works outward — never from the technology looking for somewhere to land. We publish it before we have a product because the mindset is the part that can be questioned, corrected, and adopted right now, by any family, clinician, or builder — including builders who will never use anything we make. Where a commitment below sounds like a promise, the document that enforces it is named beside it. A mindset with paperwork: that is the whole idea.

Stage, stated plainly. MAVY is at an early research stage. Nothing on this page describes a capability that exists today; it describes the commitments any MAVY technology must answer to when it does. This page is dated and versioned (v1.0, November 2026); changes will be logged in public.

✦ OneThe person is the centre; the intelligence does the adapting

Assistive technology usually asks the person to meet it halfway — learn its menus, speak its language, fit its categories. We hold the opposite: the intelligence develops around the individual — their senses, their communication, their pace — and keeps developing as they grow. If anyone is doing the adapting, it is the machine.

The paperwork: the MAVY premise, and the fact that every individual trust contract opens with a Purpose written for one specific person. MAVY →

✦ TwoTrust before capability

The terms come first: what the system may do, what it must never do, who decided, how anyone would know, and how it stops. Any assistive intelligence offered without those answers in writing, in advance, is asking for trust it has not earned — no matter how impressive the demonstration.

The paperwork: the MAVY Charter and the individual trust contract, published as drafts for comment before anything is in force.

✦ ThreeThe person keeps the decision

Support is not substitution. The system informs, sequences, reminds, and warns — and the decision word stays with the person, whose voice in their own contract grows heavier as they mature. An assistant that quietly takes decisions away is not assisting; it is replacing, one small convenience at a time.

The paperwork: the Charter's escalation and growth articles — and the standing rule in our requirements work that the system must never issue the "go" in a crossing sequence. The decision is the person's.

✦ FourSafety stands apart

The part that can hurt someone must not be the part that learns, guesses, or improvises. Safety in MAVY is a separate, deterministic layer that the intelligence cannot instruct, override, or talk its way past — and if that layer ever cannot keep a promise in the contract, that is a breach of the contract, not a bug report.

The paperwork: our safety principles, and the safety architecture specification named by version in every contract.

✦ FiveMemory belongs to the person

An intelligence that grows with someone accumulates the most intimate record imaginable. That record is the person's property, held in trust: minimal by default, processed on their own hardware wherever possible, visible to them, exportable by them, and deletable — really deletable — at their word.

The paperwork: the data covenant, Article 4 of the Charter, restated in every individual contract with the specifics filled in.

✦ SixShow your work

Criteria before experiments. Results after. Failures at the same size as passes. A record of what the system did that can be checked, not merely asserted. This applies to our research exactly as it applies to the machine: an assistive intelligence families cannot audit belongs to a laboratory culture that cannot audit itself, and we decline to be either.

The paperwork: the Charter's oversight article, and this site's programme status, kept current — including when the news is that something failed.

✦ SevenGrowth is the point; scores are not

We do not measure a developing intelligence by a percentage on a checklist. A system can be perfectly compliant and learn nothing; it can also grow in ways a static test never foresaw. What we ask of MAVY is what we would ask of any developing mind: after the environment moves, what remains — and what changes? Compliance is how we verify it kept its word. Retention and adaptation are how we will know whether it is becoming.

The paperwork: our requirements discipline, which records met and missed by name — and forbids dressing small samples in large percentages.

✦ What this is notThree fences

Not a product claim — MAVY's central capabilities have not been validated, and this page does not pretend otherwise. Not a certification — it binds us; it accredits no one, including us. And not inspiration — every commitment above either points at a document that enforces it, or it does not belong here.

Question it, correct it, or adopt it: research@mavericklabsresearch.org. Scrutiny from the community this mindset is for is not feedback to us — it is part of how the work gets done.
